Pagelark Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

In short: Pagelark works on your phone. The documents, pictures and scans you open, edit or convert are not uploaded anywhere. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking by the app.

The text-reading feature uses Google's ML Kit, which sends Google some technical information about how it ran (not your files or their text). This is explained below.

Who we are

Pagelark is an app for scanning, reading text from pictures (OCR), editing PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, and converting between them. It is made by an independent developer. You can reach us at a14049436@gmail.com.

Your files

  • Files you open, pictures you take or pick, and the text read from them are processed on your phone only.
  • While you work on a file, the app keeps a working copy in its private storage on your phone. You can clear these copies in Settings › Temporary files, and they are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Files you save, export or convert are written where the app tells you (your Downloads folder, or Pictures/Pagelark for pictures), or back to the original file when you save it.
  • The Recent files list on the home screen is stored on your phone only. You can clear it in Settings.
  • A file leaves your phone only when you choose Share, and then only to the app you pick.

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan. The photos stay on your phone.

Reading text (OCR) and technical data sent to Google

Text in pictures and scanned pages is read on your phone with Google ML Kit. Your pictures, documents and the text read from them are not sent to Google or anyone else.

ML Kit does send Google technical information so Google can find problems and measure how ML Kit is used:

  • device information, such as the phone's maker, model and Android version;
  • app information, such as the app's package name and version;
  • device or other identifiers;
  • performance and usage details, such as how long recognition took, its settings, the size of the input and output, and error codes;
  • for language detection, which language the text was found to be in (not the text itself).

Google states that this data is encrypted in transit and is not transferred to third parties. It is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. Details are on Google's ML Kit data disclosure page.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use our own analytics or tracking.
  • We do not sell or share your information.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan pages and take pictures, only when you choose to.
  • Internet: used by Google ML Kit to send the technical information described above. The app does not use the internet to send your files.

The app does not ask for access to all your photos or files. You choose each file or picture yourself with Android's file and photo pickers.

Keeping and deleting data

Everything the app stores is on your phone, and you can remove it at any time: clear temporary files and the recent files list in Settings, delete files you saved from your Downloads or Pictures folder, or uninstall the app to remove all of its own data.

Children

Pagelark is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
